Description:

The capybara is a large, social, and semi-aquatic rodent native to South America. They are friendly and docile animals, making them a great choice for exotic pet owners. Capybaras have a unique appearance with a sleek, shiny coat of fur ranging from reddish-brown to dark brown. They are excellent swimmers and require access to water to thrive. Despite their size, capybaras are easy to handle and care for, and are great for all owners.

Fresh grass, aquatic plants and fruits
